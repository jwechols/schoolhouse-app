## Mission & method [MCA]
- Mission: "Engage Minds, Pursue Christ, Discover Greatness." Classical education from a
  biblical worldview, Trivium applied in ALL subjects.
- Grammar stage (ages 5-12): children as "mental sponges" — learn facts/definitions/dates via
  memorization, chants, songs, recitation.
- Logic stage (12-13+ / gr 7+): how facts relate, cause/effect, formal logic, Latin translation.
- Rhetoric stage (16+ / gr 11-12): synthesis, eloquent expression, persuasion.
- Socratic method: dialogue not lecture; questions "create tension and stimulate thought."

## Scope & sequence (grammar stage — build to Memoria model, verify math/grammar program w/ MCA)
- **Pre-K (Lois, as Jr-K) [CC]:** letter/number recognition, handwriting readiness, oral
  language; Bible story read-alouds + short verses; counting/shapes/patterns; nursery rhymes &
  poem recitation (build the memory muscle).
- **Kindergarten (Mercy) [CC]:** daily recitation + phonics + math. Phonics: Classical Phonics
  (44 phonograms), First Start Reading; read CVC words + ~67 sight words. Handwriting: copybook
  (Scripture/poetry copywork). Math (Rod & Staff Gr 1): count/write to 100, skip-count 2s/5s/10s,
  <>=, addition facts, coins, time to half-hour, ½⅓¼. Bible stories + memory verses. Rotating:
  poetry recitation, nature study, art, music. No Latin yet.
- **3rd grade (Titus) [CC]:** peak absorption. Math (Rod & Staff Gr 3): master ×/÷ facts.
  Grammar: English Grammar Recitation (150 rules by recitation). Spelling Workout D. Composition:
  IEW paragraph writing. Latin: Latina Christiana (vocab, Latin sayings, derivatives). Literature:
  Farmer Boy, Charlotte's Web, Paddington, Mr. Popper's Penguins. Classical: D'Aulaires' Greek
  Myths (~58 myth facts). Bible: Christian Studies I (chronological). Science: animals/mammal
  orders. Geography: states & capitals.
- **6th grade (Truma) — advanced grammar [CC]:** Math: Rod & Staff Understanding Math (pre-algebra
  readiness). Grammar & Recitation III. Composition III (progymnasmata — narrative/chreia). Latin:
  Second Form Latin (full noun/verb morphology — early logic-stage thinking). History: Famous Men
  of the Middle Ages. Literature: King Arthur, Robin Hood, Adam of the Road. Bible: Christian
  Studies III (salvation history). Science: birds + intro human body.

## MCA Upper School (7-12) [MCA] — for reference if Truma is 7th / when app extends up
Singapore Math (Math in Focus) → Foerster Algebra/Pre-Calc → Euclid Geometry; Great Books primary
sources; Greek (Shuman); Logic (Custer's Introductory Logic, gr 9); Rhetoric (Aristotle's On
Rhetoric, gr 10-11); Senior Thesis. Bible each year (Pentateuch→OT→NT→World History→Apologetics).

## Memory work = the heart of grammar stage (build question banks for these) [MCA/CC]
- Bible: weekly memory verses; catechism Q&A; ~30 chronological Bible facts/yr; books of Bible in order.
- Scripture & poetry recitation (copybook passages, classic poems performed).
- Latin: declension/conjugation endings as chants; 5+ vocab words/week; sayings/derivatives.
- Math facts: skip-counting → multiplication through 15s, perfect squares 1-15², cubes 1-10³;
  US + metric conversions; geometry formulas (area/perimeter/circumference/volume); fraction-
  decimal-percent equivalents; math laws.
- History timeline: ~160 events Creation → modern, chanted; weekly "history sentences" w/ dates.
- Grammar: parts of speech, punctuation/capitalization rules, sentence structure (150 rules).
- Science: classification facts (mammal orders, bird facts, body systems), definitions.
- Geography: states & capitals; countries/features by region.
- Method to replicate in-app: repetition via chant/song/movement/games; spaced-repetition
  recitation quizzes; "Memory Master" = recite a full year's memory work.
